# WhatsApp Prescription Sharing - Quick Guide for Doctors

## 🚀 How to Share Prescriptions via WhatsApp

### Step 1: Create Prescription (Same as Before)
1. Log in to MedInfo Doctor Dashboard
2. Search and select your patient
3. Record or type the prescription
4. Review the medicines
5. Click **"Save Visit and Generate PDF"**

### Step 2: Share via WhatsApp (NEW!)
After the prescription is saved, you'll see three buttons:

1. **Download Prescription PDF** (download to your computer)
2. **View on Google Drive** (if Drive is configured)
3. **Send Prescription via WhatsApp** ⬅️ NEW!

#### To share via WhatsApp:
1. Click **"Send Prescription via WhatsApp"**
2. A dialog will ask: *"Has [Patient Name] agreed to receive their prescription through WhatsApp?"*
3. Click **OK** if patient has consented
4. WhatsApp will open in a new window with:
   - ✅ Patient's phone number (automatically filled)
   - ✅ Professional message (pre-written)
5. Download the PDF using the **"Download Prescription PDF"** button
6. In the WhatsApp window, click the attachment icon (📎)
7. Select the downloaded prescription PDF
8. Click **Send**

### ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Patient consent is required** - Always confirm the patient wants to receive prescriptions via WhatsApp
- **Manual PDF attachment** - You must download and attach the PDF yourself (WhatsApp cannot auto-attach)
- **Internet required** - Both you and the patient need internet and WhatsApp
- **Valid phone number** - Patient must have a registered 10-digit mobile number

### 📱 Supported Phone Number Formats

All these formats work correctly:
- `9876543210` (10 digits)
- `+91 9876543210` (with country code)
- `919876543210` (already has 91)
- `+91-98765-43210` (with hyphens)

The system automatically normalizes them to work with WhatsApp.

### ❓ Troubleshooting

**WhatsApp button doesn't appear?**
- Check if patient has a mobile number registered
- Verify prescription was saved successfully

**"Invalid phone number" error?**
- Update patient's mobile number
- Use a valid 10-digit Indian number

**WhatsApp doesn't open?**
- Allow popups in your browser
- Check if you have WhatsApp installed
- Verify internet connection

## ✅ Benefits of WhatsApp Sharing

1. **Fast** - Patients get prescriptions instantly
2. **Convenient** - No need to physically hand over papers
3. **Digital record** - Patient can save in WhatsApp
4. **Easy to forward** - Patient can share with family if needed
5. **Free** - No SMS charges or additional costs

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- Only authenticated doctors can access this feature
- Patient consent is required before sharing
- No sensitive medical details are exposed in the WhatsApp URL
- PDFs remain securely stored and access-controlled
- No third-party services involved (direct WhatsApp)
